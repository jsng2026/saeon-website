# SAE:ON website

Static corporate and product website for SAE:ON Pte Ltd.

## Preview

```sh
python3 -m http.server 8000
```

Open `http://localhost:8000`. There is no build step and no package dependency.

## Pages

- Corporate home and company information
- Product pages for Cushion and Ball
- Website and Cushion privacy information
- Product support guidance
- Cushion trusted-party consent endpoint

The app descriptions and visual palette are based on the corresponding product repositories. Keep product claims and the Cushion privacy policy aligned with those repositories when app behaviour changes.
