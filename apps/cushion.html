Cushion

A calmer way to build healthier browsing habits. Cushion combines a free Safari adult-site blocker with optional Spotcheck accountability shared with a trusted person.

How it works ↓

HOW IT WORKS

Support, with clear boundaries.

Cushion has two distinct parts. Its Safari content blocker helps block listed adult websites. The optional Spotcheck feature can share browsing samples and accountability alerts with a trusted person who has agreed to receive them.

Safari blocker

Helps block known adult websites using a bundled content-blocking list.

Optional Spotcheck

Shares limited browsing reports and accountability alerts with a chosen trusted person.

Explicit consent

The trusted person must accept an invitation and can unsubscribe from reports.

No screenshots

Reports may include page addresses, titles and supported search terms, but Cushion does not take screenshots.

Designed around on-device privacy

Pending captures and settings are stored on the device. Reports are submitted when Safari or Cushion next runs after the delivery time, so an inactive phone can delay delivery. The delivery relay does not intentionally store raw browsing reports, names, email addresses or page titles in its database.

Important limitation

Cushion cannot prevent its own removal, lock Safari’s system extension switches, inspect every browser, or prove that a period without a report means the device was unused.
