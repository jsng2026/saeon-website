Ball

Always tap the right card. Ball recommends which card in your collection can earn the most miles for the payment you are about to make.

How it works ↓

HOW IT WORKS

A recommendation before you pay.

Choose or search for a nearby merchant, enter the purchase amount, and Ball ranks your saved cards. Recommendations account for more than a headline earn rate and explain the factors that shaped the result.

Context-aware

Recommendations can use the merchant category, payment channel and local or foreign currency.

Cap tracking

Confirmed payments help Ball account for monthly bonus caps in later recommendations.

Net value

Miles, personal valuations and foreign-currency fees can be considered together.

Clear reasoning

The app surfaces notes such as remaining caps, applicable earn rules and estimated fees.

Built for Singapore cards

The current release is designed for Singapore credit cards billed in SGD. Card rules can refresh from a maintained catalog, while people remain able to review and adjust their own card setup.

Important limitation

iOS does not let third-party apps inspect the exact cards in Apple Wallet, observe an Apple Pay transaction or automatically switch the Wallet card. Ball makes its recommendation before payment; the person chooses the card and confirms any payment they want tracked.

Your information

Ball can be used locally without an account. It offers tools to export, restore and erase locally stored financial data. More detailed product privacy information will be published before public release.
