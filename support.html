SUPPORT

How can we help?

Find product information and the right support route for apps developed by SAE:ON Pte Ltd.

Cushion

For questions about setup, Safari extensions, Spotcheck, subscriptions or privacy, use Send feedback inside Cushion. Product details and known boundaries are available on the Cushion page.

Ball

For card-rate corrections, bugs or feature suggestions, open Ball and use Feedback in Settings. Include the card name and an issuer link when reporting an incorrect rate. Learn more on the Ball page.

Response and safety

Never include passwords, payment-card numbers, one-time codes or other secrets in a support message. Product feedback is reviewed for development and support purposes.

Availability note

Cushion and Ball are currently in development and are not yet presented on this site as publicly available App Store products.
